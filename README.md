## The fine print that decides whether BUNDLE3 saves you money

BUNDLE3 only applies when your cart contains **three or more** products drawn from six specific categories: strollers, travel systems, infant car seats, highchairs, playards, and swings.

What it excludes matters just as much:

- Toddler car seats
- Playard sheets
- Sale items (anything already discounted)
- Products shown on the site but sold by other retailers
- Phone orders entirely

There are also the standard terms Graco attaches to every offer: codes can't be combined with each other, no rain checks, good while supplies last, US only.

The practical math: if your cart has two items, the code does nothing. Either add a third item you actually need (a highchair or swing you were going to buy eventually) or skip the code and use the 15% email-signup discount instead — 15% on two items beats 0% on two items every time. If your cart mixes sale items with full-price ones, the sale items won't count toward or benefit from the bundle, which can quietly drop you below the three-eligible-items threshold.

## If you searched "graco promo code" for spray equipment

Then you're dealing with a completely different Graco — the industrial manufacturer — and its "coupon codes" work nothing like checkout codes. Delivery takes 4–6 weeks, and claims must be submitted within six months of purchase.

The currently published codes:

- **EA26** (Endurance Advantage): buy a qualifying sprayer and get a backup Endurance pump at up to 70% off — for example, $195–$245 for pumps matched to small and mid-size airless units, up to $615–$810 for large gas machines. Valid through the end of 2026, with submissions due by June 30, 2027.
- **NAT26** (National Coupon): free accessories with a qualifying sprayer purchase — mostly free RAC X spray tips, ranging from 2 tips on handheld models (about $164 in value) up to 12 tips on large gas units (up to $984). Claims due by January 15, 2027.
- **KING26**: discounted Endurance pump lowers for King-series machines, $1,300–$1,400 depending on the model.
- **ELOWER26**: a free pump lower with qualifying sprayer purchases, running August 1 through October 31, 2026.

A few restrictions apply across the board: these offers are for contractors and end users in the US, Canada, and Puerto Rico, they don't ship to P.O. boxes or dealer addresses, and Graco reserves the right to change terms at any time. None of these codes will do anything at the baby gear checkout, and BUNDLE3 won't work on a rebate form — matching the code to the right Graco is half the battle.

## Graco promo code questions, answered

**Do Graco coupon codes stack with each other?**
No. Graco's official terms state that offers cannot be combined with other offers. Pick the single best one for your cart.

**What's Graco's free shipping threshold?**
Graco's official promotion terms page lists free standard shipping on orders of $75 or more before tax, continental US only. Some coupon sites list a $40 threshold, but the official terms page is the source of truth — and the number shown in your cart at checkout overrides everything.

**Can I return something I bought with a promo code?**
Graco accepts returns within 30 days of purchase on items bought from its own site, though you cover the cost of shipping the item back. Products bought through third-party retailers can't be returned through Graco. All products carry a one-year limited warranty, and customer service is reachable at 1-800-345-4109, Monday through Friday.

**My code isn't applying. What now?**
Check four things in order: exact spelling, the three-item requirement for BUNDLE3, whether any cart item is excluded (toddler car seats, playard sheets, sale items), and whether the code has expired. If the code is valid, the discount appears the moment you click Apply — no delayed math.
